# Introduction

This report deals with investigating four companies from the automotive branch and four banks. For every sector the companies are compared to each other and an assessment about their level of being innovative is made. The companies have been selected from company.info[1]. For the automotive companies Toyota has been selected initially, after which GM, Ford and Daimler are selected from Toyota's peer group. For the banks Fortis has been chosen initially and ABN Amro, Rabobank (officially Coöp. Centrale Raiffeisen-Boerenleenbank BA) and Fortis Bank from its peer group.

In order to assess the innovativeness of the several companies, both their profile on company.info will be investigated and what is stated on the website of the corresponding company. The different criteria of innovativeness are as follows:

- The profit the company per employee, since applying innovations right should normally lead to higher productivity per employee. Innovation is often regarded as being a combination of doing something new and marketing it well. Therefore financial gain can be seen as an indicator of half of the innovation process.

- Determining whether the company did any incremental, modular, architectural and/or radical innovations recently.

- The kind of radicalness the company is bringing being the product of benefits to users, pervasiveness and improved functionality.

# Automotive Companies

Firstly the automotive companies. Looking at their profits, turnover and profit as a fraction of the turnover gives the following table:

| Company | Profit | Turnover | Profit/employee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toyota | 11 billion euro | 175 billion euro | 36 000 euro |
| GM | -21 billion euro | 101 billion euro | -87 000 euro |
| Ford | -11 billion euro | 106 billion euro | -50 000 euro |
| Daimler | 1.3 billion euro | 95 billion euro | 4 900 euro |

The first distinction that can be made here is that Toyota is doing very well, Daimler moderately well and GM and Ford very poorly. With respect to size the companies are in the same order and could thus relatively safely be compared to each other.

With respect to incremental, modular, architectural and/or radical innovations one can state in the automotive industry that only incremental and modular innovations have occurred here recently. This is due to the fact that the basic way one defines a car has not changed for quite a while (e.g. 4 wheels, driving on road box which can transport people).
