## Personal view on hackerism
In this final paragraph I will give my own opinion on hackerism. What can be seen from the paper is that many changes have happened in the past, because of the change of ethics with respect to hackers. Different kind of paradigms have been the base of these ethics, while one should think that morality is something that is relatively constant over time. The main thing one needs to ask is how to continue in the future. A starting point in this is to look at history and see how it can teach us something. Especially mistakes that have occurred might be a valuable lesson for preventing errors in the future.

What the different hacker ethics have shown us is that they didn't turn out to be sufficient. First of all they can be interpreted in many ways and second even when one has good intentions it's still hard to know how to be a good person.

I think therefore the focus shouldn't be on telling people what they can and cannot to, but in putting a focus on virtue ethics. In virtue ethics to focus is on what kind of person ought I be instead of what should I do. Especially in ICT environments this is much stronger paradigm than the hackers have used so far, because ICT is changing rapidly over time and it's almost impossible to anticipate on what's going to happen in the near future.

Practically the virtue ethics could be brought into ICT people by presenting them heroes of the past on the area of hacking. If these heroes don't cover all the skill of the personality needed, fictional characters can also play an important role. Imagine how superman and batman can stimulate children to always try to do the good.
